Create invoices and credit notes in sevdesk for your Swell orders, and book their payments. When an order is paid, its invoice is created in sevdesk with your number range and layout and arrives as paid, and when a payment is refunded, a credit note for that invoice arrives as paid out.

Connect sevdesk with your API token and choose drafts to review in sevdesk or finalized documents. Each document is listed on its order in Swell with its number, its status in sevdesk, the PDF and a link into sevdesk.

- **Invoices to the cent.** Each item, shipping and discounts are on the invoice, and its total is what the customer paid: the app compares the total of sevdesk's draft with the order before the invoice gets a number. A paid order's invoice says that it's already paid.
- **Payments booked.** Each payment of an order is booked on the sevdesk clearing account you name, with its own amount and date, and each refund as a payout. A payment method that arrives elsewhere, such as bank transfers or a PayPal account connected to sevdesk, can have another account or be left for sevdesk to match.
- **The right tax rule.** German VAT on net or gross invoices, the destination country's VAT for EU consumers under the One-Stop-Shop, for goods and for digital products, tax-free intra-community supplies and reverse charge for EU businesses with a VAT ID, tax-free exports, and invoices without VAT for small businesses (§ 19 UStG). The app uses the tax your store charged. An order that fits no sevdesk tax rule is not sent, and is listed with the reason.
- **Credit notes for refunds.** A full refund repeats the invoice's positions, and a partial refund is split over the invoice's tax rates.
- **Drafts or finalized documents.** Start with drafts, which have no number and can still be edited or deleted in sevdesk, and finalize them there or in Swell. Or create finalized documents with their number, payment booking and PDF at once. Finalized documents can be e-mailed to your customers, in German or English, with a link to the PDF.
- **Never created twice.** Each order gets one invoice and each refund one credit note, also when a request is repeated or its answer is lost. Documents sevdesk refuses are listed under Needs attention with the reason, your administrators get an e-mail, and you can retry them one by one or all at once. If sevdesk is down, the app tries again by itself for a day.
- **Invoices on demand.** Create the invoice for an order from its page, or for selected orders at once, including unpaid orders and orders from before you installed the app. When such an order is paid later, the payment is booked on its invoice.
- **One contact per customer.** A customer becomes a sevdesk contact with the first order, and later orders use it again. The app never changes an existing contact or a finalized document, and only reads your orders, payments and customers in Swell.

Setup takes a few minutes. In sevdesk, complete your company and tax settings, add a clearing account for shop payments under Bank → Verrechnungskonto, and copy your API token under Einstellungen → Benutzer. In Swell, paste the token in the app settings, run Test connection, enter the name of the clearing account, choose drafts or finalized documents, and turn on Create documents automatically. The app needs a sevdesk account with API access on sevdesk's current bookkeeping system (sevdesk-Update 2.0). sevdesk has no sandbox, so use a sevdesk trial account in your store's test environment. Orders in other currencies than EUR are skipped.
